FAQ

Questions before using FastStack?

Can FastStack manage multiple projects or tours?

Yes. Each project, tour, office fund, or petty cash group can have separate cash-in, cash-out, and balance tracking.

Where is the data stored?

FastStack uses Google Sheets as the backend database through a private Google Apps Script API. The spreadsheet is not exposed directly to users.

Can I export reports as PDF?

Yes. FastStack includes PDF report export with transaction details, project balances, summaries, and daily breakdowns.

Does the app support backups?

Yes. Daily Google Drive backups can be scheduled and retained for 30 days, with restore support from available backup snapshots.
